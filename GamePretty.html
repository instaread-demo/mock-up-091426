About the Game

Haunted Merge: Horror Story is a horror-themed merge puzzle game from WhaleJoy Game Limited. You play as an orphan raised by a wandering feng shui master who found you near a graveyard. Before he died, he gave you a locked box, the only clue to your past. Your search leads you to a run-down apartment building where the elevator opens on floors nobody pressed, empty units whisper, and a cursed construction site keeps claiming lives.

The game blends merge mechanics with a slow-burn ghost story. It’s available on Android via Google Play.
