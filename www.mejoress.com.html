Valheim 100% Achievement Guide: How to Unlock the Trophies

Valheim recently introduced its official achievement list alongside the Deep North 1.0 update, giving players fifty-three distinct reasons to return to the tenth world. Figuring out effective ways to earn these trophies gets overwhelming when you are busy surviving harsh biomes and managing basic resources. This walkthrough breaks down how to unlock each milestone and offers practical strategies to make the tough hard-mode combat challenges less frustrating.

We expect to update this living guide regularly with fresh tips as players discover more efficient completion routes.

🧠 Did You Know? The community’s dedication can get extreme: one player documented spending more than 1,600 in-game days reaching the far north before the 1.0 release. Their trip reportedly involved a road so enormous that traversing it took more than 30 minutes, illustrating just how much time some players sink into a single Valheim world.

Progression and Bosses

The core loop of the game revolves around summoning and defeating powerful entities across different environments. Progressing through the distinct biomes unlocks basic introductory trophies while setting the stage for complex combat challenges.

Summoning the Biome Rulers

Earning these accolades involves gathering designated sacrificial items and locating the corresponding altars across the world map.

  1. Eikthyr: Defeat the Lightning Stag. Place two Deer Trophies on his Meadows altar to summon him.
  2. The Elder: Defeat the Living Forest. You need to burn three Ancient Seeds at his designated altar located in the Black Forest.
  3. Bonemass: Defeat the Writhing Dead. Find his altar in the Swamp and sacrifice ten Withered Bones to spawn him.
  4. Moder: Defeat the Dragon Mother. Gather and place three Dragon Eggs at her Mountain altar to initiate the fight.
  5. Yagluth: Defeat the Twisted Soul. This Plains entity demands five Fuling Totems placed at his summoning circle.
  6. The Seeker Queen: Defeat the Crawling Matriarch. Use a Sealbreaker to access her Infested Citadel arena in the Mistlands. Her initial fight requires no sacrifice.
  7. Fader: Defeat the Emerald Flame. Search Charred Fortresses in the Ashlands to find nine Bell Fragments. Craft three Bells and ring them at his altar.
  8. Kall Fimbulbringer: Defeat the Shackled One. Bring three Malicious Blood items to his Deep North altar.

Defeating these rulers sequentially pushes your character forward into new tiers of technology, providing access to better gear for the journey ahead.

Combat Milestones and Difficulty Modifiers

Beyond the standard progression path, the remaining trophies require you to alter your combat approach or increase the global difficulty level.

  1. I Have Arrived!: Create and load into your tenth world.
  2. The First of Many: Suffer your first death and prepare to respawn.
  3. Stronger Together: Defeat any major boss while another player actively participates in the fight.
  4. Lone Wolf: Defeat any major boss solo. Eikthyr serves as a reliable option for this task.
  5. The Viking: Defeat all eight major bosses on a single character on any difficulty setting.
  6. The Tough Viking: Defeat all eight bosses on Normal difficulty or higher.
  7. The Toughest Viking: Defeat all eight bosses on Hard difficulty.
  8. Odin is Pleased: Defeat the five minibosses. You need to hunt down Brenna in the Smouldering Tombs, Geirrhafa in the Howling Caverns, Zil and Thungr in the Sealed Tower, and Lord Reto in his tomb. Zil and Thungr count as two separate enemies.
  9. The Chosen One: Assemble Dyrnwyn. Locate the Hilt, Blade, and Tip Fragments, then forge the weapon at a Level 4 Black Forge.

You can combine the normal and hard clears if you choose to increase the game difficulty early on, allowing you to unlock multiple trophies simultaneously.

Tip: Consider fighting the entry-level foes like Eikthyr to secure the solo combat accolade quickly before attempting the more complex late-game encounters.

Building Settlements

Constructing a secure base provides shelter and comfort buffs. The structural system rewards players for expanding their footprint and reaching impressive heights. Accumulating different furniture items also plays a large role in maximizing your resting bonuses.

  • The Homesteader: Construct a basic enclosed house that meets the standard shelter conditions.
  • The Architect: Build a small settlement by placing several structures together rather than stopping at a single isolated building.
  • The Builder: Place 500 individual building pieces. Standard base expansion contributes heavily to this total.
  • Comfort is King: Reach a comfort level of 20. Design a dedicated resting room and fill it with various unique comfort-providing furnishings.
  • Mighty Halls: Build upward until you hit the maximum structural height limit. You need strong support beams to achieve this.
  • Top of the World: Build a structure near the maximum world height. Starting your construction on a high mountain peak makes this achievable.
  • The Carpenter: Build the available pieces located in the Hammer menu.

Expanding your base naturally pushes your placed piece count higher over time, reducing the need to grind. Constructing the pieces from the Hammer menu demands significant material gathering, but you gain the ability to dismantle and refund those resources right afterward.

Tip: Focus on using iron-reinforced structural integrity beams when attempting to reach the vertical building restrictions.

Survival and Gathering

Sustaining your character requires continuous resource collection across the various procedurally generated landmasses. Gathering flora, mining for wealth, and accumulating raw materials unlock several important milestones.

  1. The Lumberjack: Cut down 500 trees. Use the highest tier axe available to speed up the chopping process.
  2. The Farmer: Harvest 500 crops. Replanted crops count toward this total, so maintain a regular farming cycle.
  3. The Forager: Pick 250 berries and 250 mushrooms. Search the Plains for large clusters of Cloudberries and scour the Black Forest for mushrooms.
  4. The Beekeeper: Harvest 500 honey. A single hive stores up to four units and produces one honey roughly every twenty minutes.
  5. The Survivor: Stay alive for 30 consecutive in-game days.
  6. The Treasure Hunter: Discover and open 10 buried treasure chests. Equip the Wishbone to locate the hidden loot effectively.

Passive resource generation plays a huge role in minimizing repetitive tasks. Establishing beehives early provides a steady stream of honey, while frequent farming ensures a constant supply of replantable seeds.

Tip: The foraging milestone lacks a visible Steam tracker initially. Collect the required berries first to force the in-game UI to replace the question marks with your actual mushroom progress.

Combat Challenges

Surviving the wilderness means facing off against aggressive fauna and environmental hazards. Tracking your kills and hunting down specific trophies forms a large portion of the combat milestones.

  1. The Hunter: Kill the various kinds of creatures at least once.
  2. The Master Hunter: Kill the available creature types while playing on Hard difficulty. This requirement includes passive animals.
  3. The Trophy Collector: Collect one of the available enemy trophies.
  4. Accidents Happen: Let a falling tree crush you to death.
  5. That Can’t Be an Accident: Get killed by the 10 different tree types.
  6. Are You Alright?: Suffer the possible death types in the game.
  7. Obliterated: Get killed by an Obliterator explosion. Place the device safely and stand directly next to it during activation.

Environmental deaths require deliberate planning and a willingness to lose your items temporarily. You need to intentionally place yourself in the path of falling timber from various tree varieties and stand dangerously close to active utility explosions to check off the hazard requirements.

Tip: Use the internal game menus to monitor your slain enemies and collected trophies rather than trying to memorize your progress manually.

Crafting and Cooking

Upgrading your arsenal and maintaining high health pools depend heavily on the stations you build and the recipes you discover. Expanding your culinary and blacksmithing skills rewards you with multiple accolades.

  1. The Blacksmith: Craft the different kinds of weapons. Check the crafting menus thoroughly, including late-game items and specialized station blueprints.
  2. The Artisan: Craft the items required by the achievement tracker. Wait until the late game when you possess the needed materials to attempt this.
  3. The Cook: Cook 100 dishes. Set up multiple Cooking Stations over a single campfire to process items faster.
  4. The Master Chef: Cook at least one of the available food recipes.
  5. The Gourmet: Eat the different kinds of edible items at least once.
  6. No Adventuring On an Empty Stomach: Eat 500 total food items. Keep your buffs active regularly to hit this goal naturally.

Food buffs dictate your stamina and health limits during exploration. Cooking multiple items simultaneously speeds up the preparation phase significantly, giving you more time to explore and fight.

Tip: Hold onto one copy of unusual or rare food items until you physically consume them to ensure they register for your tasting checklist.

Fishing, Exploration, and Sailing

Traveling across the oceans and mapping the environment unlocks the final set of standalone milestones. Navigation relies heavily on reading the landscape and managing your vessels effectively across long distances.

  1. The Fisherman: Catch the 12 fish types. Search the waters in the different biomes, paying special attention to the Mistlands, Ashlands, and Deep North.
  2. A Catch Worthy of Njord: Catch any four-star fish. This relies heavily on random number generation.
  3. The Legends Are True: Sail your boat over the actual edge of the world.
  4. No Time For Snoozing: Mine Abyssal Barnacles on a Leviathan’s back until it wakes up and sinks.
  5. The Explorer: Travel far toward the north, south, east, and west edges of the world map.
  6. The Pathfinder: Travel far in all four directions without opening your map. Rely on the compass and visual landmarks instead.
  7. The Sailor: Accumulate enough sailing distance to circle the world twice.
  8. The Captain: Accumulate enough sailing distance to circle the world while personally manning the helm.

Pushing the boundaries of the map carries significant risks. Sailing over the actual edge of the world usually results in losing your current boat and potentially your character inventory, so deposit your valuable gear beforehand.

Tip: Stick to the starting area waters when attempting to reel in a four-star catch, as the generation odds for high-tier fish tend to favor those initial biomes.

Mastery Unlocks

Achieving full completion triggers a final tier of automatic rewards. These act as meta-achievements for fulfilling specific grouped conditions across the other categories.

  1. Kings and Queens of Valheim: Unlock The Viking, The Hunter, The Artisan, The Master Chef, The Carpenter, and The Explorer achievements.
  2. Child of Odin: Unlock The Toughest Viking, The Master Hunter, The Survivor, and The Pathfinder achievements.

Securing these final two titles means you have successfully finished the requirements of the Viking afterlife. They require a mastery of building, combat, and survival under the harshest conditions.

Tip: Completing the hardcore creature and boss clears serves as the final and most difficult barrier for players looking to earn the Child of Odin title.

Concluding Your Journey

Taking down Kall Fimbulbringer in the Deep North usually marks the end of a long and rewarding survival campaign. Earning the final overarching meta-titles takes real effort, but keeping a structured checklist handy helps you track easily missed requirements like dying to a falling tree. Setting up dedicated outposts for your hard-mode boss fights generally reduces the frustration of sudden defeats. Following these strategies keeps your progression organized and helps you secure a completed trophy profile without unnecessary grinding.
