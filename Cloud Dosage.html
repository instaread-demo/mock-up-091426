Five New Games Join PHYND

Asterix & Obelix XXL 3: The Crystal Menhir

Asterix & Obelix XXL 3: The Crystal Menhir sends Asterix and Obelix across Roman-occupied territories as they investigate a magical Crystal Menhir and its powers. You can play by yourself or team up in co-op.

Along the way, the pair battle Romans as the Crystal Menhir mystery drives the adventure forward.

The Smurfs 2: Prisoner of the Green Stone

The Smurfs 2: Prisoner of the Green Stone sends the Smurfs out to free their village from the Green Stone’s grip. It mixes exploration, platforming, and combat across a cursed fantasy setting.

The SmurfoMix also comes along for the journey as the team works to break the Green Stone’s hold on the village.

An Interesting Journey of Monsieur PAF

An Interesting Journey of Monsieur PAF shifts the batch toward puzzle solving. The isometric adventure takes place inside an ancient temple, with obstacles standing between Monsieur PAF and the way forward.

Advertisement - Remove Ads

You’ll manipulate objects and break through obstacles as you work through the temple’s puzzles. Figuring out how to use the objects around you is a big part of getting through each area.

New Joe and Mac: Caveman Ninja

New Joe and Mac: Caveman Ninja starts with Joe and Mac’s village being raided by Neanderthals. From there, the pair fight their way through a prehistoric arcade adventure.

You can play by yourself or bring a friend along for the action.

Roto Force

Roto Force is a twin-stick shooter set in a rotating world. Fast movement and shooting drive each mission, with new weapons and boss fights mixed in along the way.

The rotation is the big hook here. The world turns around the action as you move through each mission, so you’re always adjusting to a changing orientation.

All Five Games Are Available Now on PHYND

All five games are available now on PHYND across compatible Samsung and LG Smart TVs and Amazon Fire TV devices.
