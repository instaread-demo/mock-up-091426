The New Season Edit: What to Bring Into Fall 2026

DSCENE selects fashion, accessories, beauty, and homeware for the start of the new season.

September 11, 2026
in Accessories, Fashion
0
Share on FacebookShare on Twitter

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 6:54
Instaread

The start of fall brings a natural shift in everyday routines, from what we wear and carry to the objects we keep close at home and at work. For 2026, the season calls for pieces that can adapt to changing schedules, cooler weather and longer days spent between different spaces.

FALL 2026

This year’s DSCENE edit brings together fashion, accessories, beauty, and homeware selected for everyday use throughout the new season. The focus stays on pieces that combine function with strong design and can remain useful well beyond the first weeks of fall.

440ME Fall Winter 2026

440ME’s Fall Winter 2026 collection develops the brand’s familiar silhouettes through updated fabrics, textures and seasonal variations. Signature cashmere-silk and merino-silk constructions return alongside pure cashmere and 100% wool, giving the collection a soft, refined foundation.

Specialty yarns and textured finishes introduce depth throughout the range while keeping the pieces versatile and easy to wear. Produced through 440ME’s vertically integrated manufacturing, the collection focuses on material quality, comfort and styles designed to work throughout the colder season.

Freja New York
Pure Burgundy

Freja New York introduces its Fall Winter 2026 collection with Pure Burgundy, a rich seasonal shade applied to the brand’s functional bag designs. New additions include the Elodie and Greta Totes, joined by an updated version of the Slim Clutch.

The collection also includes Cool Mauve and Saddle, a warm brick brown, giving the season a compact palette designed for everyday wear. Each color appears throughout Freja’s existing and new styles, with silhouettes suited to both daytime routines and evening plans.

ARMEDANGELS
DETOXDENIM collection

ARMEDANGELS introduces SaXcell® Lyocell to the US for the first time through its Fall Winter 2026 DETOXDENIM collection. The recycled cellulosic fiber joins the brand’s existing use of organic cotton, recycled cotton and TENCEL™ x REFIBRA™, extending its focus on lower-impact denim production.

Several men’s and women’s denim styles use a fabric made from 57% organic cotton and 43% SaXcell® Lyocell. SaXcell® combines recycled cotton waste with FSC-certified wood pulp through a closed-loop process, giving ARMEDANGELS a new material route for its long-running DETOXDENIM line.

The Horse
Tilly Bag

The Horse’s Tilly Bag in Coffee offers a compact take on the everyday leather carryall, crafted from soft pebbled leather with a clean, relaxed shape. Its streamlined silhouette sits close to the body, while the thick shoulder strap provides comfortable carrying throughout the day.

An open top with a magnetic tab keeps essentials accessible, with enough room for a water bottle, headphones and a book. An integrated keyring adds practical storage, while an embossed logo at the front keeps the exterior minimal.

KINRADEN
Doric Earrings

KINRADEN’s Doric Earring Small brings a sculptural shape to recycled sterling silver, with a hollow construction that keeps the elongated drop design lightweight. Measuring 1.5 x 3.2 cm, the earrings offer a clean geometric addition to everyday jewelry.

Part of The Stilos Collection, the earrings combine Danish design with hand-finished production. KINRADEN uses recycled sterling silver 925 for the design, with certification from the Responsible Jewellery Council.

VERAFIED
Python Éclair Bag

VERAFIED’s Python Éclair Bag, created exclusively for Selfridges as part of the Wild Things collection, features python-printed calf leather and a structured silhouette framed with metallic hardware. Two removable straps and a silver snake chain allow the bag to adapt to different styling throughout the day.

The design can be worn four ways: as a shoulder bag, top-handle bag, crossbody or clutch. Inside, a cotton twill lining houses a magnetic closure, pocket, zip compartment and key hook, balancing its distinctive exterior with practical everyday storage.

Les Composantes
Traviata Table Lamp

The Traviata Table Lamp in Moka pairs a glazed ceramic base with warm brown tones and a simple sculptural form. A brass bulb holder and sleeve introduce a subtle metallic detail, while the woven ecru cotton cable complements the natural palette.

The Sonate lampshade in Ecru completes the lamp with a matte fabric finish and softly diffused light. Made in Portugal, the lamp measures 55 cm high and 31 cm in diameter, making it a versatile choice for a bedside table, desk or living space.

Hommey Bedding Collection

Hommey brings a home-focused addition to the Back to School edit with its latest organic cotton bedding collection, designed for anyone refreshing a dorm, first apartment, or bedroom for the new term. The range introduces four striped options, Dusty Pink, Chambray Blue, Raisin Brown and Sage Stone, alongside Pink Salt and Olive in solid colorways, giving the collection enough variety to mix pieces without making the room feel overly styled.

Made from 100% organic cotton, the bedding focuses on softness, breathability and everyday comfort, while the palette adds an easy sense of personality to smaller spaces. The new collection works particularly well for students who want their room to feel considered from the start of the semester, with pieces that can stay in use well beyond the back-to-school period.

Memo Paris
Darling Point

Memo Paris introduces Darling Point, a fragrance inspired by Sydney Harbour and the warmth of the Australian coast. Created by perfumer Aliénor Massenet, the composition opens with sweet pear, apple and cinnamon, followed by jasmine, orange blossom, saffron and rose, before settling into moss, cashmere wood and praline.

The fragrance leans into a gourmand direction, with candied pear and praline bringing a rich, caramelized character to the floral heart. Artist Thomas Lélu contributes the visual identity around the launch, drawing on Darling Point’s mix of light, architecture and harbor views.

111SKIN
Everyday Skin Support Trio

111SKIN’s Everyday Skin Support Trio brings together three of the brand’s reparative products to support the complexion as part of a daily skincare routine. The set targets dryness and irritation, sensitivity and redness, as well as laxity and visible lines.

Designed for combination, dry, oily and sensitive skin, the trio focuses on maintaining skin comfort while supporting a smoother, refreshed appearance. Its multi-product format makes it a practical addition to an everyday regimen when skin needs consistent care.

Salt & Stone x Sky High Farm Goods

Salt & Stone and Sky High Farm Goods come together for Fig & Violet, a limited-edition candle inspired by the warmth of a Mediterranean summer. The scent opens with black fig, coconut milk and ivy leaves, followed by violet, white jasmine and basil, with tonka bean, orris root and white musk at the base.

The candle features artwork by Joana Avillez and retails for $49. A portion of proceeds supports Sky High Farm in the Hudson Valley, while the launch also extends to a limited Salt & Stone x Erewhon Fig & Sea Salt Smoothie, available throughout September at Erewhon locations in California.
