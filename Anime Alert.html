Will There Be a Season 2 of The Fragrant Flower Blooms With Dignity?

The first season of The Fragrant Flower Blooms With Dignity ended last month, leaving fans both satisfied and eager for more. While many were expecting a Season 2 or sequel announcement after the finale, no official updates were shared. Still, there are plenty of reasons to believe that the story of Kaoruko and Rintarou might not be over just yet.

How Much of the Manga Did the Anime Adapt?

The Fragrant Flower Blooms With Dignity anime is based on the romantic comedy manga by Saka Mikami. Season 1 adapted the story up to Chapter 40, covering the early stages of Itsuki’s and Rintarou’s growing bond. The manga, however, currently has over 160 chapters, meaning there’s more than enough source material for multiple future seasons.

If you want to continue the story right after the anime, you can start reading from Volume 6, Chapter 41.

Why Wasn’t Season 2 Announced Yet?

One possible reason The Fragrant Flower Blooms With Dignity Season 2 wasn’t announced could be related to its production studio, CloverWorks. The studio had a busy summer schedule, also handling major projects like My Dress-Up Darling Season 2 and Rascal Does Not Dream of Santa Claus.

Interestingly, all three anime premiered and ended around the same time, but only Rascal Does Not Dream of Santa Claus received a sequel announcement—a theatrical film titled Rascal Does Not Dream of a Dear Friend, officially set for 2026. And with CloverWorks currently working on Spy x Family Season 3, it’s clear they’ve had a lot on their plate, which might explain the delay in any new announcements.

Will The Fragrant Flower Blooms With Dignity Get a Season 2?

So, will The Fragrant Flower Blooms With Dignity get a second season? The chances look promising. The anime was one of the highest-rated shows of the summer on MyAnimeList, ranking above popular titles like Kaiju No. 8, Gachikuta, and Summer Hikaru Died. With strong fan support and plenty of manga content left to adapt, a sequel feels highly likely.

Even better, a special event for The Fragrant Flower Blooms With Dignity has been announced for November 1st, and events like these often bring major news. If a Season 2 announcement is coming, that’s the date to watch.

For now, fans can only wait, but all signs point toward The Fragrant Flower Blooms With Dignity returning in the near future.
