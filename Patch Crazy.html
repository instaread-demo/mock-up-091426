    There are various recordings that players must collect in the game Halloween: The Game. Players have the chance to find some of these collectibles, such as Loomis Logs and other character recordings, which may not be easily discovered while playing the story of the game. There are 14 recordings total that need to be collected. This guide covers the location of every recording and explains where to find each one.

    All Collectibles Locations In Halloween: The Game 

    14 collectible audio clips can be found throughout the game. You will have to go through the prologue and other various chapters to find them all, and many of them can easily be missed if you only focus on completing the main objectives. Most of these audio clips are found in houses or other buildings; you can find all the Loomis Logs here.

    Loomis Log #2

    The Loomis Log #2 is easily available at the beginning of the prologue. Immediately after you get control of your character, do not advance; instead, go back and head towards the corner that is farthest from where you were standing.

    Loomis Log #4

    A little further into the prologue, there is a section where you need to drop the gate in order to proceed. Turn left before heading to the gate and walk past the light. There will be a bus shelter close by. The Loomis Log #4 can be found in the trash can there.

    Loomis Log #6

    Loomis Log #6 can be found in Chapter 1. You will reach a place where there is a diner or bar. Enter the place and go towards the back part of the building, and then head for the dressing rooms located on the left side of the building.

    Loomis Log #8

    You will be given an optional objective to check out the area during Chapter 1. Moreover, you will be alerted about the mechanic. On this note, you should turn right and head into the garage. After this, you should go towards the centre of the garage and go into the office of the mechanic. There you will see Loomis Log #8.

    Loomis Log #9

    Loomis Log #9 is in Chapter 2. Go to the music shop from the map, near where Tanya is located. Go inside, then head for the rear left corner of the shop. Find the blue walls and boxes and look above the boxes for the recording.

    Loomis Log #3

    Loomis Log #3 is situated right above the cemetery. Go into the house via the front door, and then look to the right when you get in. You will immediately see the fireplace when you enter the house. Check the top of the fireplace to find the recording.

    Loomis Log #5

    Loomis Log #5 can be found in Chapter 3 after eliminating the intruder. Simply travel to the house located southeast of your current position; it will have a yellow van parked outside. Once inside the house, proceed to the bedroom; Loomis Log #5 is placed on the desk in the room near the dining room.

    Loomis Log #1

    Now in Chapter 3, go directly to the house just to the left of the house where Marcus is located. Go into the house and head towards the back of it. There you will see Loomis Log #1 on a cabinet.

    Loomis Log #10

    There are several recordings that must be collected for Chapter 4. In order to get Loomis Log #10, you have to go to the house, which is found at the centre of the map, opposite the Wallace House. You have to enter through the front door and turn left. Look for the cabinet located between the two couches.

    Loomis Log #7

    Loomis Log 7 requires that you move to the other side of the road from your previous recording location. This is an area close to the bottom part of the map, where you are expected to find Annie. You should enter the small building at the back of the house and look on top of the washing machine for the recording.

    Sheriff Brackett’s Recording

    Once you have found the former recording, go inside the house that is attached to the little guesthouse. Laurie will go outside to make a call for assistance. However, rather than crossing the street, go inside the house on the right side. Enter the front door and get into the dining room, where you will find Sheriff Brackett’s Recording on the table.

    Laurie’s Recording

    Laurie’s Recording can be found in Chapter 5. Begin the search by getting into the house from where the chapter starts, but remain on the first floor. Enter the dining room using the doors in the house. There is a table with a lamp in the room. The recording is placed on the table. 

    Loomis Log #11

    Returning from the previous collectible, move back towards the entrance of the house and exit. Move diagonally across the street towards the big white house that has a balcony and an archway. Enter via the front entrance and go up the stairs. Once you’ve taken care of the enemy at the top of the stairs, take a left and keep walking down the hallway. At the end of the hallway, walk into the room straight ahead of you. The Loomis Log #11 will be there. It’s supposed to be placed on a table but a glitch may make it float in mid-air.

    Nurse Chambers’ Recording

    For the last recording, go to the upper right corner of the map. Look for the house next to the sports arena and go inside. Enter the living room, where you will see a big China cabinet opposite the fireplace. The recording of Nurse Chambers is on top of the China cabinet.
